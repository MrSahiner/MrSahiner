# Hi, I'm Salim

Backend-focused full-stack developer and final-year Computer Engineering student at Yozgat Bozok University (graduating 2027). I build products end to end, from requirements analysis and architecture to CI/CD and release.

## What I'm working on

I'm founding **Grentra** and building two products on my own:

- **[Coffee Go](https://mrsahiner.github.io/en/projects/coffee-go/)**: multilingual QR menus, verified reviews, loyalty cards and table ordering for cafés and restaurants. My graduation project.
  `.NET 10` · `PostgreSQL/PostGIS` · `Redis` · `React Native` · `React` · 470+ automated tests
- **[KutliGo](https://mrsahiner.github.io/en/projects/kutligo/)**: digital invitations and a QR photo album for weddings and engagements. MVP in December 2026.
  `Next.js` · `ASP.NET Core` · `PostgreSQL` · `Redis` · `Playwright`

Previously: software engineering intern and Scrum Master at Intellium Bilişim Teknolojileri (summer 2026).

## How I work

- **Analysis first:** requirements, architecture and risks are written down before code.
- **Security from day one:** authorization on the server, secrets out of the code, personal data protected.
- **Small, reviewable steps:** every change on its own branch, with tests, ready for review.

## Code

Product repositories are private. As an open sample, I published a simplified version of Coffee Go's translation pipeline:

**[menu-translation-pipeline](https://github.com/MrSahiner/menu-translation-pipeline)**: on-demand translation with a city-aware glossary, two-level cache (HybridCache + Redis), owner corrections that are never overwritten, and integration tests on real PostgreSQL and Redis with Testcontainers.
